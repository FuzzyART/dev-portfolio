Portfolio Website

This repository contains my personal portfolio website.

The site is currently in an early stage of development. At the moment, it serves primarily as a digital business card with basic information about me and ways to get in touch.

Over time, I will expand it with technical projects, interactive demonstrations, and blog posts documenting my work, experiments, and lessons learned in areas such as Rust, WebAssembly, machine learning, Linux, and self-hosted infrastructure.

Rather than treating this as a finished product, I consider it an evolving engineering portfolio that grows alongside my projects.
